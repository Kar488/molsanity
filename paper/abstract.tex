\small
\textbf{Abstract.}
Feature attributions are routinely used to justify molecular graph neural
network (GNN) predictions to chemists, yet they are almost never audited for
\emph{reliability}: existing evaluation frameworks ask whether an explanation
is faithful to the model, not whether the model's explanation can be trusted,
and not where it stops being trustworthy under the scaffold shift that
characterises real drug-discovery workflows. We present \textbf{MolSanity}, a
reliability-audit framework that wraps canonical attribution implementations
(Captum, PyTorch Geometric, RDKit) rather than proposing a new attributor, and
scores every (dataset × backbone × attributor × split) cell on six axes:
motif-native coherence, occlusion--attribution faithfulness, ground-truth
localisation where node labels exist, cross-checkpoint stability, calibration
linkage, and confidence/correctness regime stratification. Our central finding
is that \emph{faithfulness is not correctness}, and that the two carry no
dependable relationship in either regime. Across $\nSelectionTests{}$ selection
tests --- $\nArms{}$ molecular ground-truth arms $\times$ two splits $\times$
$\mutShiftNMetrics{}$ ranking metrics, with dataset, backbone and attributor
set held fixed within an arm --- a faithfulness-only ranking picks an
attributor other than the ground-truth-best one in $\nSelMismatch{}$, and this
is no less true in distribution ($\nSelMismatchInd{}$ of $\nSelTestsInd{}$)
than under scaffold shift ($\nSelMismatchShift{}$ of $\nSelTestsShift{}$). The
sharpest case is MUTAG under a Bemis--Murcko scaffold split, where all
$\mutShiftNMetrics{}$ metrics miss. The occlusion measure selects
\mutShiftPickOccspearman{}, whose agreement with the mutagenic nitro motif is
GT AUROC $\mutShiftPickGtOccspearman{}$ --- near-perfectly \emph{anti}-aligned,
and sixth of the $\mutShiftNAttr{}$ attributors audited --- while the ground
truth ranks \mutShiftGtBest{} best at $\mutShiftGtBestVal{}$ in the audited
seed, $\mutShiftGtBestSeedMean{}\pm\mutShiftGtBestSeedSd{}$ across
$\nPooledSeeds{}$ (median paired gap
$\mutShiftGapOccspearman{}$ AUROC, Wilcoxon $p\mutShiftPOccspearman{}$,
Benjamini--Hochberg $q\mutShiftQOccspearman{}$ over the $\nSelectionTests{}$
selection tests). Pooled over all $\nPooledCells{}$ cells of the
$\nPooledArms{}$ molecular ground-truth arms (each an across-seed mean of
$\nPooledSeeds{}$ seeds), the faithfulness--correctness rank correlation is
$+\pooledIndRho{}$ in distribution ($p=\pooledIndP{}$) and $\pooledShiftRho{}$
under shift ($p=\pooledShiftP{}$) --- neither distinguishable from zero. Per
arm under shift it runs from $\armShiftRhoMin{}$ on \armShiftRhoMinDataset{} to
$+\armShiftRhoMax{}$ on \armShiftRhoMaxDataset{}, so reliability is a property
of the (dataset, backbone, attributor, split) cell rather than of the
attributor, and no pooled coefficient describes any of the arms. Restricted to
the $\nPooledPriorArms{}$ arms that predate two externally authored molecular
rationale benchmarks, the same computation gives $\pooledPriorShiftRho{}$ at
$p=\pooledPriorShiftP{}$; adding those benchmarks removes the effect, and the
$\nPooledArms{}$-arm figure is the one we report.
Faithfulness itself does not fall under shift --- across the
same $\nPooledCells{}$ cells it \emph{rises}, from $\pooledIndFaith{}$ to
$\pooledShiftFaith{}$ ($p=\pooledShiftFaithP{}$), while ground-truth
localisation does not move ($\pooledIndGt{}$ to $\pooledShiftGt{}$,
$p=\pooledShiftGtP{}$). The metric therefore moves in the reassuring direction
across a shift that leaves correctness unchanged, and gives no warning either
way. Using \nRecords{} committed
per-molecule records we further find that on confidently-wrong predictions
ground-truth localisation degrades ($\regCCGt{}$ to $\regCEGt{}$, $n=\nRegCEGt{}$)
while their measured faithfulness \emph{improves} ($\regCCOcc{}$ to
$\regCEOcc{}$) and their stability is no worse --- both proxies point away from
the failure --- and that the calibration--reliability link attenuates from a
per-cell median of $\medCalibRho{}$ to $\pooledCalibRho{}$ when cells are
pooled, a Simpson's-paradox trap for anyone reporting a single aggregate
number. Results are the committed \texttt{\runConfig{}} run: \nDone{} of
\nAttempted{} cell-runs completed. Every number, figure and table regenerates
from the committed artifacts.
