\textbf{Abstract.}
Feature attributions are routinely used to justify molecular graph neural
network (GNN) predictions to chemists, yet they are almost never audited for
\emph{reliability}: existing frameworks ask whether an explanation is faithful
to the model, not whether it identifies the chemistry that determines the
property, nor where it stops being trustworthy under scaffold shift.
\textbf{MolSanity} is a reliability-audit framework that wraps canonical
implementations (Captum, PyTorch Geometric, RDKit) rather than proposing a new
attributor, scoring every (dataset × backbone × attributor × split) cell on six
axes: motif-native coherence, occlusion--attribution faithfulness, ground-truth
localisation where node labels exist, cross-checkpoint stability, calibration
linkage, and confidence/correctness regime stratification.
Our central finding is that \emph{faithfulness is not correctness}, and that
the two carry no dependable relationship in either regime. Across
$\nSelectionTests{}$ selection tests --- $\nArms{}$ molecular ground-truth arms
$\times$ two splits $\times$ $\mutShiftNMetrics{}$ ranking metrics --- a
faithfulness-only ranking picks an attributor other than the ground-truth-best
one in $\nSelMismatch{}$, and this is no less true in distribution
($\nSelMismatchInd{}$ of $\nSelTestsInd{}$) than under scaffold shift
($\nSelMismatchShift{}$ of $\nSelTestsShift{}$); on MUTAG under shift it
prefers an attributor anti-aligned with the nitro motif (GT AUROC
$\mutShiftPickGtOccspearman{}$) over one at $\mutShiftGtBestVal{}$. Pooled over
$\nPooledCells{}$ cells the
faithfulness--correctness rank correlation is $+\pooledIndRho{}$ in
distribution ($p=\pooledIndP{}$) and $\pooledShiftRho{}$ under shift
($p=\pooledShiftP{}$), neither distinguishable from zero, while per arm under
shift it runs from $\armShiftRhoMin{}$ to $+\armShiftRhoMax{}$: reliability is
a property of the individual (dataset, backbone, attributor, split) cell rather
than of the attributor. Restricted to the $\nPooledPriorArms{}$ arms of an
earlier analysis it gives $\pooledPriorShiftRho{}$
($p=\pooledPriorShiftP{}$); two externally authored rationale benchmarks remove
the effect, and we report the $\nPooledArms{}$-arm result.
Faithfulness itself does not fall under shift --- it \emph{rises}, from
$\pooledIndFaith{}$ to $\pooledShiftFaith{}$, while ground-truth localisation
does not move --- so the metric gives no warning either way. Over \nRecords{}
per-molecule records, localisation degrades on confidently-wrong predictions
($\regCCGt{}$ to $\regCEGt{}$) while their measured faithfulness
\emph{improves}, and the calibration--reliability link attenuates from a
per-cell median of $\medCalibRho{}$ to $\pooledCalibRho{}$ when cells are
pooled. Every number, figure and table regenerates from the committed
artifacts.
