# Supplementary material

Generated by `paper/make_supplementary.py` from the committed reports under `results/`. Every file here is a byte-for-byte copy; nothing in this folder is written by hand.

The full pipeline, configurations, trained checkpoints and per-molecule audit records are at <https://github.com/Kar488/molsanity> under the MIT licence.

## `SEED_VARIANCE.md`

Across-seed mean, standard deviation, min and max for every cell run under more than one seed. Read this against any attributor ranking in the manuscript: an effect smaller than the spread here is not evidence. It is the companion to the single-seed selection tests.

## `BENCHMARK_GT.md`

The faithfulness-versus-ground-truth selection experiment as the pipeline emitted it, including the per-cell conclusions generated from the computed results.

## `BENCHMARK.md`

Head-to-head attributor comparison over the full matrix, including the framework metrics (Fidelity+/-, characterisation, unfaithfulness) reproduced from the canonical implementations.

## `RATIONALE_USE.md`

The rationale-use test: occluding the ground-truth substructure and measuring whether the prediction collapses, which is what separates 'the attribution is wrong' from 'the model uses a different rationale'.

## `ABSTENTION.md`

Coverage-reliability curves for selective prediction on explanations -- what an audit buys a practitioner who can abstain.

## `LIMITATIONS.md`

The maintained limitations register, longer than the manuscript's limitations section and including items that did not survive editing.
